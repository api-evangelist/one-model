# One Model

> One Model is an HR / people-analytics software platform that unifies fragmented workforce data (HRIS, ATS, payroll, engagement, performance, finance) into a governed data mesh and applies AI to generate insights for HR leaders and executives. Products include Data Mesh, Data Stories, One AI (predictive modeling + natural-language assistant), and enterprise AI connections (including an MCP Server Access add-on) that let governed people data feed tools like ChatGPT, Copilot, and Claude.

## Products
- [One AI](https://www.onemodel.co/products/one-ai): AI-powered predictive people-analytics modeling and a natural-language assistant.
- [Data Mesh](https://www.onemodel.co/products/data-mesh): unify, govern, and activate workforce data across HR, Finance, and operations systems.
- [One AI Data Intelligence](https://www.onemodel.co/products/one-ai-data-intelligence): agentic AI over governed people data.
